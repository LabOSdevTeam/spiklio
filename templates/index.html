FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms can I connect?

Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp and Telegram. You can run several at once and manage them from one dashboard.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You connect a channel, write instructions in plain language and the bot is live.

Can the AI collect names and phone numbers?

Yes. It picks up names, phones and plans from the conversation and saves them to your CRM and your Google Sheet.

What language does the bot speak?

It follows the client's language unless your instructions fix a specific one.

Can I control what the bot says?

Yes. System prompts, creativity level and extra reminders let you set tone, rules and boundaries.
